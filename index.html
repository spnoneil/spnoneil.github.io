Every world has bugs. My job is finding them before the players do. I'm a QE engineer in Portland, currently a QA Analyst at PlusQA, a software testing company with a massive device library, where I've spent 4+ years testing web, iOS, Android, Windows, and macOS releases.

I work both sides of quality. Hands-on: coordinating releases, exploratory and new-feature testing, regression passes, and bug triage. In code: Python and TypeScript suites in Selenium, Cypress, and Robot Framework, wired into CI/CD. Knowing when a person's judgment beats a script, and when it doesn't, is most of the job.

I came to software after a decade of customer-facing work, and it still shapes how I test: I think about the person on the other side of the screen, then document exactly how things break so developers can fix them fast. I lean on AI where it helps: Claude for test case generation, testing support, and script drafting, and Gemini for Google docs and summaries, with a human check on every result.
